BAPP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BAPP, operated by Kerry Tooley ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the BAPP mobile application ("the App"). BAPP is a tool for conducting and recording professional bat surveys.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by BAPP, operated by Kerry Tooley, United Kingdom. For any privacy questions, contact us at support@bapp-app.com. We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

2. Information we collect

The App collects and stores the following information that you enter or capture:

  • Survey data — survey details, locations, surveyor entries, and bat observations you record.
  • Photographs — images you capture or select (e.g. equipment setup, property views, site boundaries) using your device camera or photo library.
  • Location information you enter — survey site locations and boundaries that you provide as part of a survey. The App does not track your device's background GPS location.
  • Access codes — the survey code used to identify a surveyor and load the correct survey.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, and the App does not contain third-party advertising.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera — used only to take photographs for a survey.
  • Photo library — used only to let you attach existing images to a survey.

These permissions are used solely for the purposes above and only when you choose to add a photo.

4. How we use your information

We use the information to:

  • Provide the App's core function of recording, storing, and exporting bat survey data.
  • Sync your data across sessions and devices associated with a survey.
  • Generate survey reports and exports that you initiate.

5. Where your data is stored

Survey data and photographs are stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), operated by Google. Data may be processed on Google's servers. Data is also cached locally on your device to support offline use. See Google's privacy information at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase, as our hosting and storage processor.
  • Other users of the App. Survey records and photographs are held in a shared backend that is not currently partitioned per survey, so another user of the App may be able to view survey data that you record. Do not record data in the App that you are not willing to share with other users, including sensitive roost locations, until per-survey access controls are in place.
  • Authorities or third parties where required by law.

7. Photographs of third-party property

Surveys may involve photographing buildings or land. You are responsible for ensuring you have any necessary permission to capture and store such images, and for complying with applicable privacy and data-protection laws when doing so.

8. Data retention

We retain survey data for as long as your account or survey remains active, or as needed to provide the App. You may request deletion of your data as described below.

9. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, under UK GDPR / GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email support@bapp-app.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your survey data and associated photographs, contact support@bapp-app.com and we will action your request within 30 days.

11. Children

The App is intended for professional use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
